Good to know

Your questions

Do you do custom designs, and can I bring my own?

Yes, I create custom fine line tattoo designs in North London. You are welcome to bring your own ideas or reference images, and I will adapt them to suit the placement you want. We talk through the details before I start.

How small can a fine line tattoo be?

Fine line tattoos can be very small, from around 0.5 cm, depending on the design. If a design has a lot of detail, I may suggest making it a little larger or simplifying it, so it stays clear as it heals.

Does a fine line tattoo hurt?

It depends on where the tattoo goes and on your own pain threshold. Small fine line tattoos are usually easy to deal with and quick to complete. Areas with thinner skin or close to the bone, such as the ribs, ankles or inner arm, tend to feel sharper.

Do fine line tattoos fade?

Yes, they can. The lines are thinner and less ink goes into the skin than with bolder styles, so sun, friction and time show on a fine line tattoo sooner. That is why aftercare matters so much. Keep it out of direct sun while it heals, and once it has healed, use sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher) whenever it is exposed.
