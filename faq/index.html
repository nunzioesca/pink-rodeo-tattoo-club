About fine line tattoos

What is a fine line tattoo?

Fine line tattooing uses delicate, precise lines to create minimalist designs. It suits small pieces such as lettering, flowers and meaningful symbols, and has a light, understated look on the skin.

How long do fine line tattoos last?

A fine line tattoo can last a lifetime, and how crisp it stays depends a lot on how you look after it and where it is placed. Over the years some lines may lighten, which is normal for this style.

Do fine line tattoos fade?

Yes, they can. The lines are thinner and less ink goes into the skin than with bolder styles, so sun, friction and time show on a fine line tattoo sooner. That is why aftercare matters so much. Keep it out of direct sun while it heals, and once it has healed, use sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher) whenever it is exposed.

What are the downsides of fine line tattoos?

Fine lines soften sooner than bold ones, and tiny details can lose definition. Very small designs with a lot of detail are affected most, because the lines have little room to spread. The right placement and good aftercare reduce this.

Will I need a touch-up?

Not always. Fine lines heal differently on every skin, and sometimes an area comes out lighter than expected. If we agree a touch-up is needed, it is free when done within 30 days of your appointment.

A touch-up does not make a tattoo darker or help it last longer. It is only useful when an area really needs it, so I avoid tattooing the same skin twice without a reason. Get in touch as soon as your tattoo has healed.

How small can a fine line tattoo be?

Fine line tattoos can be very small, from around 0.5 cm, depending on the design. If a design has a lot of detail, I may suggest making it a little larger or simplifying it, so it stays clear as it heals.

Which parts of the body suit fine line tattoos best?

The forearm, upper arm and shoulder are popular choices. Fingers, hands and other areas that rub against clothing or get a lot of sun may fade faster. The best placement depends on your taste, the design and your lifestyle, and we choose it together.

Does a fine line tattoo hurt?

It depends on where the tattoo goes and on your own pain threshold. Small fine line tattoos are usually easy to deal with and quick to complete. Areas with thinner skin or close to the bone, such as the ribs, ankles or inner arm, tend to feel sharper.

Your appointment

What should I expect at my first tattoo appointment?

We start by talking through your design and the placement, and I make sure you are happy with both before I begin. I guide you through the process and tell you what to expect while I tattoo. At the end I explain the aftercare, so you know how to look after your tattoo at home.

How should I prepare for my fine line tattoo appointment?

Sleep well the night before, eat before you come, drink plenty of water and wear comfortable clothing that lets me reach the area. Please avoid alcohol before your appointment. It thins the blood, so you bleed more while I tattoo.

Healing and aftercare

How should I look after my fine line tattoo?

Follow the aftercare I give you closely. For the first 24 hours, keep the tattoo wrapped in cling film while it is still very fresh. During that time, wash the tattoo gently with a neutral soap, and change the cling film every time you wash it: pat the tattoo dry, apply a thin layer of panthenol cream or Vaseline and wrap it in fresh cling film. Never reuse the cling film or leave the same piece on for the whole 24 hours, because bacteria can grow underneath it and cause an infection.

After the first 24 hours you no longer need the cling film. Keep using a thin layer of cream until the end of the first week, and for longer if the tattoo still looks dry. Avoid scratching, picking and direct sun while it heals. Once it has healed, sunscreen helps stop the lines fading and protects the tattoo from strong light, which can spoil the final result. If the area gets more red, swollen or painful after the first few days, or you feel unwell, message me or speak to your GP.

How long should I keep second skin on a fine line tattoo?

If I cover your tattoo with a second skin, you do not need any cream at first. I apply the first dressing at your appointment, and it stays on for 24 hours. Then you take it off and put on the second one, which I give you. That one stays on for 48 hours. After that, remove it completely and start with a thin layer of panthenol cream or Vaseline.

How long does a fine line tattoo take to heal?

The surface of the skin usually heals in 2 to 4 weeks. Complete healing can take longer, depending on your skin and aftercare. A tattoo often looks a little lighter once healed than it did when fresh, because new skin grows over it.

When can I swim, go to the gym or use a sauna after a fine line tattoo?

Once the surface has healed, which takes 2 to 4 weeks, you can go back to your usual routine, including swimming, the gym and the sauna. Until then, avoid anything that makes you sweat a lot or rubs the area, and keep it out of pools, baths and the sea.
